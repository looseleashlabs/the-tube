The Tube — Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

The Tube is a personal, household web app that shows your own YouTube subscriptions as a retro cable-TV guide. It has no server, no accounts of its own, no ads, and no analytics. Everything runs in your browser.

What it accesses, and only when you approve it

  • YouTube (read-only): your subscription list and those channels' public uploads, to build the guide. Videos play in YouTube's official embedded player.
  • Google Drive (only files The Tube creates): two files in your own Drive — your household's channels, lists and watch progress ("The Tube – household sync") and a cache of creators' recent uploads ("The Tube – library"). The Tube cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • YouTube (manage), optional: only if you press a Subscribe button, to add that subscription.

Each of these is a separate Google sign-in that you approve with a code, and each can be signed out in The Tube's Setup screen or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Where your data lives

In your browser's local storage on each device, and in the two Drive files above, in your own Google account. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server run by The Tube.

Other services

  • YouTube / Google — for sign-in, the data above and video playback, under Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • SponsorBlock (sponsor skipping) — The Tube asks it for sponsor segments using only the first four characters of a scrambled video id, so it never learns which video you are watching.

Google API data

The Tube's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide The Tube's features to you, is never sold, and is never shared with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Sign out in Setup (or remove access at the link above), clear the site's data in your browser, and delete the two Drive files. That removes everything.
